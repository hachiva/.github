# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

Hachiva is committed to maintaining a respectful, professional, and constructive environment.

Expected behavior includes:

- Communicating respectfully and constructively
- Providing useful and actionable feedback
- Respecting different perspectives and experiences
- Focusing discussions on the project and the work
- Protecting private, confidential, and sensitive information

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks
- Insulting, inflammatory, or deliberately disruptive comments
- Publishing another person's private information without permission
- Spam, manipulation, or repeated off-topic activity
- Any conduct that creates an unsafe or unprofessional environment

## Reporting

Concerns about conduct should be reported privately to:

**hello@hachiva.com**

Do not report sensitive conduct matters through public issues, discussions, or pull requests.

Reports will be reviewed privately and handled as fairly and appropriately as possible.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other content that violates these standards.

Participants who engage in unacceptable behavior may receive a warning, temporary restriction, or permanent removal from Hachiva-managed spaces.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to Hachiva repositories, communication channels, and other project-related spaces.
